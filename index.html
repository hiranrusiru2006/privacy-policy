Privacy Policy

ET Panthiya.lk Quiz Portal Mobile Application

Last updated: June 15, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how ET Panthiya.lk ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

When you register and use our App, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Exam year
  • District
  • WhatsApp number
  • Firebase authentication ID

Usage Data

We automatically collect:

  • Quiz attempts and results
  • Time spent on each quiz
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • IP address
  • App crash reports

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  • Providing and maintaining our App
  • Personalizing your learning experience
  • Analyzing quiz performance and improving content
  • Communicating with you about updates and support
  • Preventing fraud and ensuring security
  • Complying with legal obligations

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Firebase services:

  • Authentication data is stored in Firebase Authentication
  • Quiz results and user profiles are stored in Firestore
  • We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data
  • All data transmissions are encrypted using SSL/TLS

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Service providers (Firebase/Google Cloud) who assist in app operation
  • Educational authorities if required for certification purposes
  • Law enforcement when required by law

Aggregated, anonymized data may be used for research and statistical analysis.

5. Your Data Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing
  • Export your quiz history

To exercise these rights, contact us at the information below.

6. Children's Privacy

Our App is intended for students preparing for technical education exams. While we don't specifically target children under 13, we:

  • Do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13
  • Encourage parents to monitor their children's app usage
  • Will delete any data discovered to be from children under 13

7. International Data Transfers

As we use Firebase services, your data may be processed in the United States or other locations where Firebase operates. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for these transfers.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of changes by:

  • Posting the new policy in the App
  • Sending an in-app notification
  • Updating the "Last updated" date

Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
